We propose a comprehensive DL keyword taxonomy to provide a clear and systematic picture of the diverse DL research landscape. Structured as a hierarchical framework, it organizes <a href="/call/" target="_blank">topics</a> into a shared vocabulary for the community.

Initially proposed during the business meeting at DL 2026 in Lisbon, this taxonomy serves a dual purpose. For researchers, it offers a broader perspective on the field, helping them situate their own work within the larger DL ecosystem and recognize connections across subdisciplines. For DL Program Committee Chairs, it streamlines the assignment of submitted papers to coherent sessions.

We intend for this taxonomy to evolve with the community, adapting to new research directions while maintaining a structured overview of the field. For feedback and corrections, please contact <a href="https://lat.inf.tu-dresden.de/~alisa/" target="_blank">Alisa Kovtunova</a>.
